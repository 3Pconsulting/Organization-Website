The method · Lean, in plain words

Big companies hunt 8 kinds of waste. Your business has the same 8.

Fortune 500 companies use Lean to find waste and remove it. Most small businesses never get it. Here's what each one looks like in a business like yours.

D · DefectsRedoing work

Wrong quote, wrong address, wrong part. You pay twice for one job.

O · OverproductionDoing more than asked

Reports nobody reads. Custom proposals for people who never buy.

W · WaitingMoney stuck waiting

Jobs done, invoices not sent. Customers waiting on a call back.

N · Non-used talentYou doing $15 tasks

The most valuable person in the business does data entry at night.

T · TransportationInfo moving around

Text to notebook to spreadsheet to invoice. Every hand-off drops something.

I · InventoryPiles of unfinished

Unanswered leads, half-done quotes, an inbox that never empties.

M · MotionHunting for things

Searching for the file, the login, the price you quoted last time.

E · Extra processingTyping it twice

The same customer details entered into three apps by hand.

Quick answers

Before you start

Do I need employees for this to help?

No. Most Florida businesses have none. For a solo owner the fix is automation and written steps, so the business keeps moving when you're on a job, sick or on vacation.

Do I have to buy new software?

Usually not. We start with the tools you already pay for. If a new tool clearly pays for itself, we show you the math first and you decide.

I'm not technical. Will I be able to run it?

Yes. Everything comes with plain written steps and a recorded walkthrough you can rewatch.

Is this in person or online?

Online, by video and screen share, anywhere in Florida. North Central Florida businesses can ask about an on-site visit.

What's the difference between a single fix and the 7-Day Build?

A single fix solves one problem, like missed leads. The 7-Day Build maps your whole flow from first call to getting paid and fixes up to three problems at once, with automations included.

Can it text my customers back?

Yes, as an add-on. Phone carriers require business texting to be registered first, and their approval can take several days to a few weeks. We build everything else while we wait, and switch texting on once it's approved.

What can slow down the 7 days?

The clock starts once we have your logins and intake answers. Approvals from outside companies, like carrier texting registration, can take extra time. We tell you up front if anything in your setup will.

How do I pay?

The 7-Day Build is paid before the work starts. Monthly Help is billed each month. After you send the form, 3P confirms your start date and sends a secure payment link.
