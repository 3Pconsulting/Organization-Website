What Working With 3P Actually Looks Like

Not the five-step process — the actual experience. What it feels like to be a client, from the first message to the finished fix.

3-minute read · For owners who want to know what they’re actually getting into

No sales pressure. No jargon you need a translator for.

You won’t get a 45-minute pitch call before anyone tells you what anything costs. Pricing is public, the scope is written down before you pay, and the language is plain — if a recommendation doesn’t make sense in one read, that’s on the explanation, not on you.

The standard is simple: if you can’t explain what changed and why to someone else in your business, the work isn’t finished yet.

What you can actually expect

Direct communication, not a ticket queue

Talk through your business and the agreed work directly with 3P, with clear explanations of the recommendations and next steps.

A defined timeline, held to

Your written assessment plan within 1 week after the required review or session and complete intake, stated upfront — not “soon” or “when we get to it.”

No disappearing after payment

The Clear Deliverable Guarantee means if something in the agreed scope is missing or doesn’t match what was promised, it gets corrected at no additional charge — not a policy on paper, an actual standard the work is held to.

What this isn’t

It isn’t a subscription you forget you’re paying for. It isn’t vague advice with no deliverable at the end. It isn’t hourly billing where the incentive runs against you. One focused assessment, one fixed price, one personalized 30-day action plan — that’s the whole model.

Still growing — and that’s worth knowing

3P is early. That means direct, personal attention on every engagement right now — not a large firm passing you between account managers. As the client list grows, that’s the standard being built to protect, not one that fades once there are more clients to serve.
