Complete a short questionnaire and provide whatever files or access the fix requires. This is the only real work on your end.

You receive the final deliverables, a walkthrough, and a next-step recommendation. Delivered in 1 week, start to finish.

What if my situation is more complicated?

Added workflows, departments, records, integrations, or ongoing work are quoted separately before they begin — the original scope never quietly expands on you.

What if I'm not sure I even need consulting?

This is real operational consulting, done by someone who’s done this work at a high level — the difference is how it’s delivered: a defined service, a specific deliverable, a price, and a completion point. Not open-ended hourly billing that never quite finishes.

The Clear Deliverable Guarantee

3P guarantees delivery of the written deliverables listed in the agreed scope. If something included is missing or doesn’t match the approved scope, it gets corrected at no additional charge. That’s the actual promise — not vague reassurance, a specific commitment.
