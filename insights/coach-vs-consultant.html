Business Coach vs. Business Consultant: What’s the Difference?

You know something needs to change. What you don’t know is whether you need a cheerleader or a mechanic — and paying for the wrong one wastes months you don’t have.

4-minute read · For owners deciding where to spend their next dollar of help

The short answer

A coach works on you. A consultant works on the business. Both have real value — they just fix completely different things, and buying the wrong one is how owners end up frustrated and no closer to fixed.

One question cuts through most of the confusion: are you looking for someone to help you think differently, or someone to actually fix what’s broken and hand you a working result?

What a business coach actually does

Mindset, accountability, direction

A good coach helps you set goals, think through decisions, stay accountable, and grow as a leader. That work is real and valuable — but it’s about you, and it’s open-ended by design. There’s rarely a defined deliverable or a moment where the engagement is simply “done.”

What a business consultant actually does

Diagnosis, a fix, a result

A consultant looks at a specific operational leak — missed leads, late invoices, a process that keeps breaking — and builds and installs an actual fix. There’s a defined scope, a specific deliverable, and a point where the work is finished and something concretely changed.

The test that actually matters

Coaching changes how you think. Consulting changes what’s actually happening in the business. If your leads are dying in silence or your invoices go out late, no amount of mindset work fixes that — the process itself has to change, which is consulting work, not coaching work.

Three questions to know which one you need

  1. Can you name the specific thing costing you, or does it feel more like general overwhelm?
  2. Do you want a defined deliverable and an end point, or ongoing accountability and conversation?
  3. Would fixing this require changing a process, or changing how you personally approach things?
