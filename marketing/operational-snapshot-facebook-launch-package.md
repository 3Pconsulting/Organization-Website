# 3P Operational Snapshot — Facebook Launch Package

Final launch-ready package for the 3P Help Me Operational Snapshot Facebook campaign.

This document supplements `marketing/operational-snapshot-facebook-campaign.md` and records the final creative direction, caption, campaign objective, funnel, and asset requirements selected for launch.

## Campaign objective

**Primary marketing goal: Lead generation.**

The campaign is not optimized primarily for likes, engagement, page follows, general awareness, or website traffic.

The desired conversion is:

> **A completed Operational Snapshot from a business owner who provides contact information.**

The business goal is to generate qualified business leads through completed Operational Snapshots, then follow up with the businesses where 3P identifies a worthwhile service opportunity.

### Funnel

**Facebook ad/post → Operational Snapshot → contact information → completed Snapshot → 3P review → qualified follow-up → potential client**

The Operational Snapshot is the single campaign destination.

## Facebook / Meta objective

When setting up the campaign, select the option closest to:

**Leads / website conversions**

Optimize toward completion of the Operational Snapshot rather than post engagement or general page traffic.

If Meta requires a conversion event, the preferred event is the completed Snapshot / successful Snapshot submission.

## Final Facebook caption

Keep the caption intentionally short because the graphic carries the primary message.

> **Your business should work for you—not the other way around.**  
> **Take 60 seconds to uncover more time, profit, and control.**

Do not add a long explanation beneath this unless campaign testing shows a clear need.

## Final creative copy

### Main headline

**DO YOU OWN A BUSINESS?**

### Secondary headline

**Or does your business own you.**

The first statement is visually dominant. The second statement is smaller so the two ideas are clearly separated.

### Benefit bullets

Use exactly three:

1. **UNCOVER LOST PROFITS**
2. **RECLAIM YOUR TIME**
3. **STREAMLINE OPERATIONS**

These intentionally cover three different value areas:

- Profit / financial opportunity
- Owner time / relief
- Operational improvement

### Upper-right 3P motif

Use:

- **PEOPLE**
- **PROCESS**
- **PROFITS**

These appear in the connected/orbital background treatment.

### CTA

**GET YOUR FREE OPERATIONAL SNAPSHOT**

## Removed copy

The following support line was deliberately removed from the final creative:

> Build a business that works for you.

The graphic is stronger and cleaner without it.

Do not automatically reintroduce this line into the launch creative.

## Visual direction

Use the established 3P visual system from `marketing/brand-guide.md`.

Final creative characteristics:

- Deep navy background
- Gold accents
- Premium business-intelligence / operational-diagnostic feel
- Actual 3P Help Me logo, not a recreated substitute
- Laptop showing an Operational Snapshot / business dashboard
- Thin gold border / framing
- Connected-node / orbital visual language
- No stock-photo people
- No cartoon graphics
- No generic bright-blue SaaS aesthetic
- No unnecessary filler copy
- High contrast and crisp typography

## Logo usage

Use **one** real 3P logo per creative.

The horizontal 3P Help Me lockup is preferred when the layout allows it because it provides the clearest brand identification.

Do not use both the horizontal and circular logos in the same ad unless a future design specifically requires both.

## Asset sizes

### Primary Facebook feed / mobile asset

**4:5 vertical**

Recommended export size:

**1080 × 1350 px**

This is the primary creative because most Facebook traffic is expected to be mobile and the 4:5 format occupies more vertical screen space in-feed.

Text must remain readable at phone size.

### Desktop / wide companion asset

**16:9 horizontal**

Recommended export size:

**1920 × 1080 px**

Use for placements or contexts where a landscape asset is appropriate.

The desktop version should retain the same copy, visual language, CTA, and hierarchy as the mobile version.

## Mobile readability rule

The campaign graphic must be understandable without zooming.

Priority hierarchy:

1. **DO YOU OWN A BUSINESS?**
2. **Or does your business own you.**
3. Three benefit bullets
4. CTA
5. Laptop/dashboard as visual proof/context

Small dashboard text is decorative context and must never carry essential campaign information.

## Messaging strategy

The campaign should lead with potential and benefit rather than directly telling owners that their business is broken.

The creative allows the owner to recognize the problem on their own.

Preferred benefit framing:

- More time
- More profit
- More control
- Better operations

Avoid overusing generic words such as:

- clarity
- stronger
- brighter tomorrow
- chaos
- efficiency

Do not stack multiple versions of the same promise into one graphic.

## What qualifies as a lead

A useful campaign lead is not simply someone who clicks the ad.

A qualified campaign action is a business owner who:

1. Enters the Operational Snapshot.
2. Answers the essential questions.
3. Provides contact information.
4. Completes the Snapshot.
5. Gives 3P enough information to identify whether a meaningful service opportunity exists.

## Follow-up purpose

The Snapshot is not intended to force every respondent into a sales call.

3P reviews the completed Snapshot and identifies opportunities that are genuinely worthwhile.

Follow-up should connect the respondent's results to relevant 3P services, including areas such as:

- Profit / cost opportunity
- Owner time recovery
- Process improvement
- Operations streamlining
- Systems / automation / integration
- On-demand operational support

## Launch rule

The image does most of the selling.

Keep the Facebook post copy short.

Do not turn the caption into a long consulting explanation.

Primary launch caption:

> **Your business should work for you—not the other way around.**  
> **Take 60 seconds to uncover more time, profit, and control.**

Primary CTA:

> **GET YOUR FREE OPERATIONAL SNAPSHOT**
