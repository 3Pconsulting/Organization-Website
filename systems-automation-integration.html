A Business Fix assesses one focused area and gives you a personalized 30-day action plan. Systems Design & Improvement coordinates the processes, responsibilities and tools within an agreed scope.

Who implements the design?

Implement the design yourself, hire another qualified provider, or ask 3P for a separate implementation quote. You own the design and can choose who builds it.

What does the starting price cover?

Systems Design & Improvement starts at $1,997 as a one-time design fee. We agree your scope, deliverables, timeline and final price before work begins. Implementation is quoted separately.

How long will it take?

The design timeline is agreed for your scope before work begins. It does not carry a fixed weekly deadline.

What about software and extra changes?

Software, subscriptions and provider fees are separate unless expressly included in your quote. Additional work requires a separate quote and your agreement.
