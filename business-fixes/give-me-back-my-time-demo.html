2 of 4 · What 3P Found

Mike is the bottleneck because the system requires Mike.

The goal isn't to tell Mike to “manage his time better.” We identify why routine work keeps requiring the owner.

Customer calls→MIKE→Constant interruptions
Estimate requests→MIKE→Slow response
Tech questions→MIKE→Owner dependency
Job completion→MIKE→Invoices delayed
Follow-up needed→MIKE→Leads can disappear
Diagnosis: several recurring tasks do not require owner judgment; they require clearer routing, standards, decision rules, and automation where appropriate.
3 of 4 · The Fix

Remove Mike from the work that doesn't need Mike.

3P maps what to keep, delegate, standardize, automate, combine, delay or stop—then prioritizes the changes with the highest practical value.

Before

  1. Lead comes in → Mike
  2. Mike creates estimate
  3. Mike schedules job
  4. Tech calls Mike for common decisions
  5. Mike creates invoice
  6. Mike remembers follow-up

Target operating model

  1. Lead routed to defined owner/team
  2. Standardized estimate template
  3. Scheduling follows defined process
  4. Technician decision matrix handles routine questions
  5. Job completion triggers invoicing workflow
  6. Follow-up uses reminders/automation where appropriate
