Questions

Quick answers

Can you replace an employee?

We can take on defined work and recurring tasks. First we help you see whether you need a hire, short-term help, or a better process.

Can we start with one small project?

Yes. A focused project is often the best way to see what the business needs.

Will I need to buy new software?

Not automatically. We start with what you already use, and suggest new tools only when the value is worth the cost.

Do you work remotely?

Yes. Most work is done remotely through shared files, video calls, email and access you approve.

How is the price set?

Defined projects are quoted by scope. Cleanup sprints are priced by the amount and state of the work. Recurring help is a monthly scope with stated limits. Extra needs are discussed before they become extra charges.
