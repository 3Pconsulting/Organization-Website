Quick answers

How is this different from a Business Fix?

A Business Fix is one need, one price, 1 week. These options are for work that spans the whole business, repeats every month, or needs someone embedded. Bigger scope, still a set price agreed before work starts.

Do these still have set prices?

Yes, in the way that matters: no open-ended hourly billing. The exact number comes after a short scoping call, because it depends on how many systems, how much training, or how many months are involved.

Can I start smaller and grow into this?

That's the normal path. Most of these talks start after a Business Fix or On-Demand Support already handled the first need and showed a bigger one.

Will I need to buy new software?

Not automatically. We use the simplest tool that works, and suggest new software only once the process and the real need are clear.
