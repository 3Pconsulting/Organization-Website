COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you reach out

How is this different from a Business Fix?

A Business Fix is one problem, one price, one week. These options are for when the need spans the whole operation, repeats every month, or requires someone embedded — bigger scope, still a defined price agreed before work starts.

Do these still have fixed prices?

Yes, in the sense that matters — no open-ended hourly billing. At this scale the exact number comes after a short scoping call rather than being published sight-unseen, because it depends on how many systems, how much training, or how many months are involved.

Can I start smaller and grow into this?

That's the normal path. Most of these conversations start after a Business Fix or On-Demand Support engagement already solved the first problem and surfaced a bigger one.

Will I need to buy new software?

Not automatically. 3P uses the simplest workable tool and does not recommend new software until the process and actual requirement are clear.
