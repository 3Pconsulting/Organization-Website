Quick answers

Advisory or system maintenance?

Monthly Advisory is $750/month for guidance. System Maintenance starts at $200/month for the agreed implemented system. They are scoped separately; monitoring, response expectations and corrective-work allowance must be agreed before a maintenance contract starts.

Does advisory include hands-on work?

No. Implementation, setup, cleanup and new improvements are separately scoped and quoted.

Can you add features under maintenance?

Maintenance preserves or restores the agreed operation of an implemented system. New features, additional automations and material redesign are separately quoted work.
